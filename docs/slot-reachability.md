# Slot reachability of the open allocator rows

Generated by `tools/slotmap.py --all`. No build required; both bodies are
read from the **linked** images (ours at the reccmp report's `recomp`
address, retail at its `address`), because for a COMDAT row the object's
copy need not be the one the linker kept.

## The question

Wave 7 established, on two rows by hand, that a colour row is fundable
only if its divergent frame slot is a **named local on both sides**. If the
divergence is the placement of a **compiler temporary**, or a coalescing
decision, then no declaration-level lever -- declaration order, block scope,
an unused padding local -- can name it, because a temporary has no source
identity. This screens every open row on that question.

**55 of 81 open rows already have retail's exact

`~MxStreamController` method answers -- barely arises across the pile.

## The REACHABLE list (the work queue)

### `0x1006b140` LegoAnimPresenter::CopyTransform — 0.8149

5 of 6 divergent slot(s) aligned, non-overlapping, >=3 accesses. Permutation:

```
  ebp-0x0014 -> ebp-0x0090   w4  x6   delta -124
  ebp-0x0024 -> ebp-0x0020   w4  x5   delta +4
  ebp-0x0020 -> ebp-0x001c   w4  x3   delta +4
  ebp-0x0090 -> ebp-0x0024   w4  x2   delta +108
  ebp-0x001c -> ebp-0x0018   w4  x8   delta +4
  ebp-0x0018 -> ebp-0x0014   w4  x7   delta +4
```

`mn` is the divergent named local: shallow for us at `ebp-0x14`, deep for
retail at `ebp-0x90`, six accesses, and the disp8-vs-disp32 cost of that is
exactly the row's -7 bytes. The four `+4` shifts are consequent. **Wave 7
already tried the obvious lever on it -- six declaration orders, all of
which left the body at 941 bytes and all of which scored worse than
baseline** -- so this row is fundable in principle but its first lever is
spent.

## UNREACHABLE, with the evidence

- **`0x100bb1d0` MxDisplaySurface::VTable0x30** (0.8611) — all 2 divergent slot(s) touched < 3x -- spill-shaped
- **`0x100aa510` LegoLOD::Read** (0.7268) — ours ebp-0x43 is 4B, not 4-aligned; ours ebp-0x43(+4) partially overlaps ebp-0x40(+4); ours ebp-0x44(+4) partially overlaps ebp-0x43(+4)

`LegoLOD::Read` is the worked example from wave 7: retail coalesces
`numNormals` (live only before the mesh loop) with `numPolys` (live only
inside it) into partially overlapping slots, and places the 3-byte
`GetColor()` temporary where we put an unaligned 4-byte slot.

## AMBIGUOUS, by cause

| cause | rows |
|---|---:|
| bodies too different to trust a permutation (<80% aligned) | 13 |
| slots contested or claimed twice after majority resolution | 9 |

These are **not** verdicts of unreachability. They are rows where the
screen declines to answer, which is the honest outcome for a contested
slot -- a confident answer there would be the wrong one.

## How a verdict is computed

1. Align the two bodies at SHAPE level (registers and frame displacements
   erased) so the alignment survives a permutation of the frame.
2. Read the ebp/esp displacement off **both** sides of every aligned pair.
   `ebp` locals are negative; a frameless function's frame is `esp`-relative
   and positive, and `esp` displacements are canonicalised by tracking
   esp's delta from entry, or the same variable aliases across push depth.
3. Resolve each slot by supported majority (winner must double the
   runner-up); contested slots force AMBIGUOUS.
4. Drop singleton pairings that collide with a well-supported one as
   alignment noise -- counted in the reason, never silent.
5. Classify: partial overlap or an unaligned 4-byte slot is a packer
   artefact; a divergent slot touched fewer than three times is
   spill-shaped; anything else that is aligned and non-overlapping is a
   candidate named local.

Both wave-7 hand results are reproduced exactly by the tool, which is the
regression test: `0x1006b140` REACHABLE with the `mn` permutation, and
`0x100aa510` UNREACHABLE on the overlap.
